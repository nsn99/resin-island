ISOLÉ / VOL. 001

A world made of code.

This is a real 3D scene, not a moving image. Terrain, trees, rocks and water are generated procedurally. The portable edition uses WebGL 2, with GPU height-field ripples and screen-space refraction.

Move over the sea to make ripples. Click for a splash. Drag to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Space pauses motion; R resets the scene.

Caustics and optics are real-time visual approximations. Frame rate depends on the device. Nothing is uploaded; this file works offline.
